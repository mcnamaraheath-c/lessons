Merchant's Ledger

How did I do?
Every merchant started with 60 coins.
I finished with coins.
Circle one and fill in the blank:
A I finished with MORE than 60.   My coins − 60 = coins earned
B I finished with FEWER than 60.   60 − my coins = coins lost
Example: a merchant who finished with 118 coins writes 118 − 60 = 58 coins earned.
Think and write
1. Silk cost 8 coins in Chang'an and 90 in Constantinople. In your own words, why did the price grow at every stop?
2. Vandak said: “I have never seen China. My silk has.” What did he mean?
3. Would you rather be a merchant who runs one leg of the road, or the whole road like you just did? Why?
Exit ticket
Name one thing that traveled the Silk Road that was not a good for sale, and tell how it traveled.
